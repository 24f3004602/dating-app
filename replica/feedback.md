# What users of mainstream dating apps struggle with

Date: 2026-11-04. Method: replica-entrepreneur steps 2-3, adapted (decision
D004). **Evidence base:** two U.S. surveys (Pew 2023; Forbes Health and
OnePoll 2024, n = 1,000), one regulator settlement (FTC, 2025), one pricing
study covering six countries including India (2022), company disclosures, and
one Indian market report. **No review corpus was collected yet**, so there are
0 individual reviews and no verbatim quotes. Source numbers refer to the
Sources table in `recon.md`.

Strength: **strong** = large or independent data plus a visible industry
response; **medium** = one solid source; **thin** = inferred or single-source.

## 1. What they hate

| # | problem | evidence | strength |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Harassment and unwanted sexual content, worst for women | 38% of U.S. online daters received unwanted explicit messages or images; 56% of women under 50. 30% were contacted again after saying no [18]. All three apps ship nudges, blurring and filters [2][7][13]. | strong |
| H2 | Scams, fake profiles and bots | 52% believe they met a scammer; 40% say companies do a bad job on fake accounts versus 20% good [18]. The industry is moving to mandatory liveness checks and AI detection [3][7]. Trust is the top issue in India [21]. | strong |
| H3 | Exhausting, low-yield experience (burnout) | 78% report burnout at least sometimes; the top cause is not finding a good connection (40%) [19]. Products are pivoting to curated daily picks and fewer, better conversations [4][8][11]. | medium |
| H4 | Lopsided attention | Women are overwhelmed by messages: about 1 in 5 often, roughly 3 times the rate of men. 55% of past-year users have felt insecure about getting too few messages, men far more often than women [18]. In India women are about 26% of users, get excess attention, and men struggle for visibility [21]. | strong |
| H5 | Billing and subscription practices | FTC settlement of $14M (2025) over hard cancellation, undisclosed guarantee conditions and lockouts after billing disputes. Match says these practices are outdated [17]. A 2022 study found price variation by age for the same plan, including in India [20]. | medium |
| H6 | Unanswered matches and ghosting | Hinge built Your Turn Limits because many matches never got a reply, and says replies rose in tests [11]. Ghosting is cited among burnout drivers [19]. | medium |
| H7 | Paying to be seen, or to see who likes you | The tier structures gate visibility and the likes list [5][6][10]. No user-sentiment data was collected for this theme. | thin |

## 2. What is missing (requested by name)

Only Bumble's own commissioned survey (6,138 women aged 23-35, worldwide,
April 2024 [8]) names wants:

- more ways to start a conversation: 46%
- knowing dating intentions is very important: 77%
- many struggle with people who are vague about intentions: 68%

No review-board or forum requests were collected (thin).

## 3. What is unsolved (positioning seeds)

1. **Serious, intent-first dating in India that respects privacy and family
   context.** Today this is split between global swipe apps and matrimony
   sites [14][21].
2. **Safety and fairness that do not depend on paying.** Visibility is sold
   while harassment and scams remain the top harms [5][10][18].
3. **Honest pricing**: one published price per market and plan, cancellation
   as easy as signing up [17][20].
