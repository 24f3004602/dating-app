### Screening evidence (2026-11-05)

Domains were checked through the registries' public RDAP servers (IANA
bootstrap): Verisign for .com, NIXI for .in, Google for .app. A "free" result
(RDAP 404) means not registered today; the registrar still has the final say
(premium or reserved names). App Store: Apple's public search API, India and US
storefronts. Play: the public search and listing pages, India.

**Haanji: fails screening.**
- [Radio Haanji](https://haanji.com.au/) (HAANJI PTY LTD, Australia; apps on both stores since 2018) runs **"Haanji Rishte"**, which it calls its matrimonial platform. Its iOS app added Haanji Rishte in version 11.0.0 (January 2025, per App Store release notes found by search). That's the same service class as ours (45).
- haanji.app (registered 2026-05-01): "Haanji: One tap. Your people know you're alive.", a coming-soon daily check-in app for "friends, families, couples, and small groups", contact hello@haanji.com. That's a social app in classes 9 and 45.
- **Taken:** haanji.com (2006), haanji.in (2005), gethaanji.com (2026-06-17), gethaanji.in (2026-09-07), and every hanji domain.
- **Free:** sayhaanji, haanjiapp (.com, .in, .app).

**Vaada: no dating conflict found, few domains.**
- **Taken:**
  - vaada.com (2004; serves a bare page titled "vaada.com"; expires 2026-11-22);
  - vaada.in (2026-01-12; a silver coins and gifts shop, with the "Vaada Jewel" iOS app by EDJOBSYNC ENTERPRISES);
  - waada.com, waada.in and waada.app.
- **Play apps:** "Vaada" (vaada.co, by Site Guru, purpose not read); "Vaada: Promise Tracker" (Big Helpers). The matrimony app VadarVadhuvar appears in the same search (the prefix noted in stage 3).
- A search result showed www.vaada.app as a shopkeepers' barter network, but that host doesn't resolve and the domain is unregistered.
- **Free:** vaada.app, getvaada, vaadaapp (.com, .in, .app).
- **Risk:** a common word (weak distinctiveness).
